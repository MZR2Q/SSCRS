Help

Frequently asked

How do I become a member?

Choose a category — affiliate, regular or honorary — and apply online or by form. The Board reviews applications monthly.

What does membership include?

Access to educational activities, member rates at the Annual Forum, voting rights for regular members, and a listing in the members directory.

When is the Annual Forum?

15–17 April 2026 in Riyadh — three days of keynote lectures, live surgery demonstrations and hands-on workshops.

How are committees formed?

Under Chapter 5 of the Basic Bylaw, the Board and the General Assembly form standing and temporary committees of three to five members.
