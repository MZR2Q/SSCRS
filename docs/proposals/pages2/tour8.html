Frequently asked

How do I become a member?

Choose a category — affiliate, regular or honorary — and apply online or by form. The Board reviews applications monthly.

What does membership include?

Educational activities, member rates at the Annual Forum, voting rights and a directory listing.

When is the Annual Forum?

15–17 April 2026 in Riyadh — lectures, live surgery and workshops.
