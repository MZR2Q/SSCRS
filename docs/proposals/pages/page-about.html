About the Society

A national home for colorectal surgical excellence

Founded by decree of the Saudi Commission for Health Specialties, SSCRS was established to organize scientific work, strengthen professional fellowship, and elevate public awareness of colorectal health.

Four pillars

We serve as the premier platform connecting specialists, trainees, researchers, and healthcare partners — fostering a community dedicated to the highest standards of patient care.
